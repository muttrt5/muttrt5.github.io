Refund Policy

We make returns simple and stress-free for all PetAura customers.

Returns

You can request a return within 14 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, in original condition, and in original packaging.

Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within a few business days.

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven’t received your refund yet, check your bank account first, then contact your payment provider as processing time may vary.

Contact

For any refund-related questions, contact us at: support@petaura.com
