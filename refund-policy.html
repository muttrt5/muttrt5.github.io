Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy, we're here to help.

Returns

You have 14 days to request a return after receiving your item.

To be eligible for a return:

  • The item must be unused and in the same condition that you received it.
  • It must also be in the original packaging.

Refunds

Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you about the status of your refund.

If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within a few days.

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven’t received a refund yet, please check your bank account again.

Then contact your payment provider, it may take some time before your refund is officially posted.
